# Known issues and verification boundaries

This candidate is not a validated raw-data release. Worksheet exports preserve the supplied values.

1. **BBR arithmetic:** For the 40% PUP row, S=113.6 MPa and m=0.384 imply S/m=295.833333 MPa. The stored S/m is 259.8 MPa. Determine which measurement or derived value is erroneous; do not silently overwrite it. The ranking remains lowest at 40% using either ratio.
2. **Phenol reaction enthalpy:** `reaction_thermodynamics` stores -8.875 kcal/mol, whereas Table 6 and prose report -8.785 kcal/mol. Primary thermochemical output is needed to determine the correct value. Gibbs-energy summaries and the rounded barrier summaries match.
3. **Storage top/bottom labels:** Origin column B is labelled top and contains 70.20, 71.10, 71.55, 71.70 degrees C; D is labelled bottom and contains 70.75, 71.35, 71.80, 71.90. Table 9 assigns these series oppositely. Absolute differences agree. Check specimen records before choosing labels.
4. **Digitized coordinates versus primary outputs:** All four rheological master-curve projects and the density, specific-volume, and energy-path projects contain `DigiData` image-coordinate sheets. They cannot validate the manuscript claim that the deposited values are primary simulations or measured DSR data. Document the original image and calculation/measurement source.
5. **Rheological internal consistency:** A log-log interpolation screen in the common frequency range gives loss/complex modulus ratios up to approximately 2.04 for 30% PUP (32 of 63 compared coordinates exceed 1.15). For same-state linear viscoelastic data, G'' cannot exceed |G*|. This is a diagnostic on independently digitized curves, not an exact pointwise comparison of original measurements. Recheck sample assignments, scales, raw DSR data, fitting, and G'=|G*|cos(delta), G''=|G*|sin(delta).
6. **Specific-volume/Tg reconstruction:** Fitting the low-branch 3 and high-branch 7 digitized coordinates gives an intersection near 240.7 K; the two explicitly stored line segments intersect near 246.9 K. Neither reproduces the stated 253 K directly. Obtain original equilibrated values and regression output. The coordinates themselves are not exactly the stated temperature schedule.
7. **Density range:** As an exploratory screen, points at time coordinates >=20 ps span about 0.9436-1.2127 g/cm3 (mean about 1.1001); 195 of 694 are outside 1.05-1.15. Twenty ps is not an author-confirmed equilibration cut-off. The manuscript's stated fluctuation interval needs qualification or verification from original MD outputs.
8. **Viscosity dosage trend:** At 175 degrees C the stored PUP viscosities are 0.49, 0.45, 0.35 Pa s for 30%, 40%, 50%, respectively. Thus a uniform increase with dosage over the full range is not supported. At 135 degrees C they are 1.23, 1.47, 1.50. The separate 19-point curve contains irregular temperature coordinates and must be identified as measured, digitized, interpolated, or fitted.
9. **Viscosity acquisition and fit:** The two-temperature worksheet and the 19-point curve do not have identical temperature coordinates or values. Several auxiliary straight-line fits produce negative viscosities on extrapolation. These are retained as auxiliary outputs and must not be treated as measurements or extrapolated construction windows. The base-asphalt 135-degree summary value 1.06 Pa s also differs from Table 1's 413 labelled Pa s; identify the specimen and verify unit/source.
10. **Construction temperature claim:** A 20-30-degree reduction requires a specified viscosity criterion and common comparison protocol. These are not supplied. A fitted curve alone does not establish mixing/paving/compaction temperatures or field workability.
11. **FTIR provenance and intensities:** Spectra have no sample labels or units in their worksheet headers, include ordinates above 100 and an initial zero, and the graphs are labelled transmittance (%). The graphs contain vertical offsets. Preserve the values and confirm original file assignments and processing before interpreting absorption-intensity changes quantitatively. Figure labels contain PUMB/90# aliases; some assigned peak positions differ between figures, peak-finder output, and prose (e.g. 1221.3 versus about 1225 cm^-1).
12. **Error bars:** MSCR and mixture-property error columns exist, but SD/SE/CI, replicate counts, and calculations are not provided. No statistical interpretation has been assigned.
13. **Missing measurements:** Table 10 mass changes and Table 12 flexural tensile strengths are not in the supplied result sheets; neither are replicate-level records. These table columns remain unverified.
14. **Mixture controls and labels:** PUMB versus PUPMA labels are not documented. The RAP content of separate PUMB and SBS controls and the mass basis of RAP percentage are unspecified. Do not compare or pool the groups until the mixture designs are described.
15. **Optimal binder content:** An extra worksheet is supplied, with asphalt/aggregate ratios 1.4-3.4, but no sample/RAP group identification. Its target mixture and ratio basis need confirmation before use as supporting mixture-design evidence.
16. **Primary model and reaction verification:** OPJU projects do not resolve the formula/count mismatch in manuscript Table 5, the PPG/TDI chain construction, or the reaction-equation notation. Origin figure data cannot substitute for model coordinates and primary quantum-chemistry outputs.
17. **License and release identifiers:** License, repository URL, DOI, and validated release version are pending. Do not assert unrestricted reuse or public availability before the corresponding actions are completed.

The accompanying consistency_checks.csv compares only available numerical fields. Matching rounded values establish agreement with the manuscript, not authenticity, repeatability, or validity of underlying measurements. Exact numerical exports and unchanged OPJU copies were validated.
